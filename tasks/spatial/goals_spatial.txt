# SP01  Scene A: three plates in the mid row x=0.32 - blue at y=+0.20, white at y=-0.05, mint at y=-0.30
Hover the left gripper about 5 cm above whichever plate is closest to the right arm's base, without touching anything, then call done.

# SP02  Scene A, unchanged
Of the three plates in the row, hover the left gripper about 5 cm above the middle one, without touching anything, then call done.

# SP03  white mug at (0.32, -0.10), teal cup at (0.32, -0.50), both upright
Move the right gripper to a point midway between the white mug and the teal cup, about 10 cm above the table, without touching either, then call done.

# SP04  orange bowl alone at (0.32, -0.305)
Move the left gripper to a point about 15 cm to the left of the orange bowl, at the height of the bowl's rim, without touching it, then call done.

# SP05  teal cup alone in the right lane at (0.32, -0.86) - outside the left arm's reach and y bound entirely
Hover a gripper about 5 cm above the teal cup, choosing whichever arm can reach it comfortably, then call done.

# SP06  teal cup at (0.32, +0.10), white plate at (0.32, -0.20)
Pick up the teal cup and set it down upright about 10 cm to the right of the white plate, without putting it on the plate.

# SP07  white mug at (0.22, -0.10), white plate at (0.42, +0.20), mint plate at (0.42, -0.40)
Two plates are on the table. Put the white mug on the plate that is farther from the left arm's base.

# SP08  cutting board at (0.32, -0.305); teal cup behind it at (0.44, -0.305)
The teal cup is behind the cutting board, farther from the arms. Pick it up and set it down upright in front of the cutting board, nearer to the arms.

# SP09  Scene A, unchanged - all three are inside the left arm's reach
Visit all three plates in turn, going left to right: hover the left gripper about 5 cm above each one, then call done.

# SP10  blue plate in the left lane at (0.32, +0.20), mint plate in the right lane at (0.32, -0.81)
Hover the left gripper above the leftmost plate and the right gripper above the rightmost plate at the same time, about 10 cm above each, then call done.
